# VCE Drama, Theatre Studies, Dance (written exams + performance exams)

Sources: VCAA exam specifications (written and performance), 2025 and 2024 external assessment reports. My own summary.
**This skill can mark written answers and Statements of Intention / Interpretation Statements. It can't watch a performance**, but it can critique a performance *plan*, script or description against the criteria.

## Drama (Study Design from 2025)
**Written exam:** 15 min reading + 1 h 30 min writing. Three sections: A (a play from the playlist, Unit 3 Outcome 3), B (devised ensemble performance, with stimulus insert, 25 marks), C (devised solo, with stimulus). The "Terms used in this study" section (pp. 12–16) is examinable.
- Write about a **specific moment** and a **specific actor**. Describe how space, production areas and performance skills create a **specific impact on the audience**. Keep contextual detail only as long as it justifies the point.
- **Evaluate** = how effectively two production areas were applied or manipulated to communicate meaning, with a judgement.
- **Analyse** the actor–audience relationship as a deliberate choice, plus another named skill.
- Use the study design's terminology (expressive skills, performance skills, dramatic elements, conventions, production areas) precisely.

**Solo performance exam (7 min):** you *must* present one prescribed structure, or every criterion gets zero. Criteria cover compliance with all aspects of the structure, research, scripting/editing, performance style, technical aspects, and the **four expressive skills equally (voice, movement, gesture, facial expression)**.
- Common faults: under-edited, cluttered or rushed scripts. Performance style not evident. Purposeless use of space or facing away from assessors. Recreating ads or characters without a clear purpose.
- Statement of Intention: don't copy out the structure. Use the word limit to guide assessors on interpretation and choices.

## Theatre Studies (Study Design from 2025)
**Written exam:** 15 min reading + 1 h 30 min writing. Section A (30): unseen script excerpt + dramaturgy insert (no prior knowledge needed). Section B: Unit 3 AOS3 (the production they saw/analysed). Section C: Unit 4 AOS3.
- Interpret the unseen script through production roles with justified, specific choices. Use the dramaturgy as evidence.
- Analyse and evaluate a production with specific moments and named production roles.

**Monologue exam:** interpret one prescribed monologue (or score zero) in two production roles (acting + directing, or two design roles). Quote directly from **two or more sections** of the monologue in the Interpretation Statement.
- High scorers: a well-informed **concept** applied consistently and justified orally. Contexts of the monologue conveyed through the role. Theatre style(s) applied deliberately. Elements of theatre composition integrated. A clearly identified **intended audience**. Dramaturgical research evident. Decisions linked across both stages.
- Low scorers: limited evidence of working in the selected role. Missed task requirements.

## Dance (Study Design from 2019)
**Written exam:** 15 min reading + 1 h 30 min writing. 80 marks. Unit 3 AOS1 + Unit 4 AOS1 (analysis of works) make up 70–80%, and the student's own solos and group work make up 20–30%. Scenario questions on choreographic processes, safe rehearsal and performance.
- Name the **form** of the composition solo accurately (binary AB, ternary ABA, rondo ABACA, etc.), lay out its sections and state the intention.
- **Discuss production elements** in depth: exactly *how* the element communicated the intention, not just a description.
- Documenting personal movement vocabulary: two plausible approaches and how each was used.
- **Safe dance practices** applied to the scenario (warm-up, hydration, appropriate surfaces, injury prevention, rest).
- Analysis of works: use the study design terms (elements of movement: time, space, energy; movement categories; physical skills; choreographic devices; intention) with specific examples from the work.

**Solo performance (2.5–5 min):** criteria (equally weighted) cover control of the eight physical skills across the six movement categories, manipulation of time, space and energy, whole-body projection and artistry, and performance practices tied to the **Statement of Intention**.
