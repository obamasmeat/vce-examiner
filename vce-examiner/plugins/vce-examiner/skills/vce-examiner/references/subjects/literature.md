# VCE Literature (Study Design from 2023)

Sources: VCAA exam specifications, criteria and expected qualities, 2025 and 2024 external assessment reports. My own summary.

## Exam format
- 15 min reading + 2 hours writing. Two Task Books. Students write on **two texts from different categories** (novel, play, short stories, other literature, poetry).
- **Section A – Developing interpretations (20):** one set passage from one text.
  - **Q1 (6 marks):** the significance of the set passage *in the context of the whole text*.
  - **Q2 (14 marks):** a key concept, using the passage **and** the whole text: the ideas, views and values it raises, and how the text **endorses, challenges and/or marginalises** them.
- **Section B – Close analysis (20):** a sustained interpretation of a different text, built on detailed analysis of **two or more of three set passages**.
- Equal weighting, so spend equal time on each section. There's no word limit.

## Criteria and what top-band looks like (paraphrased)
**A Q1:** a complex, perceptive exploration of *why the passage matters to the whole text* (plot, character, structure, ideas, form, context), with detailed evidence and sophisticated expression.
**A Q2:** a complex interpretation centred on *the named concept*. A thorough evaluation of the ideas, views and values foregrounded, and of how they're endorsed, challenged or marginalised.
**B:** a relevant, plausible interpretation. Analysis of the set passages/key moments. **Close analysis of language and literary features** (syntax, imagery, tone, structure, form) and how they build the interpretation.
Low bands: summary or paraphrase of the text, treating the passage as self-contained, little textual evidence.

## Marking habits to replicate
- **Paraphrasing a passage scores very few marks.** Every section requires close analysis of how language makes meaning.
- Q1: explain *how and why* the passage functions within the broader text. A standalone reading of the extract is capped.
- Q2: the concept must **shape the whole response**. Use the passage as a departure point, then range across the text. **Don't repeat Q1's points.** Avoid formulaic structures: let endorsement/challenge arise organically from the analysis.
- Section B: don't analyse each passage in isolation (especially poetry or short stories). Use the passages as a springboard to a sustained interpretation of the *whole* text, linking ideas and language.
- Integrate short quotations seamlessly as part of the analysis, not as illustration alone.
- Accurate literary metalanguage with controlled expression. Complexity for its own sake doesn't help.
- **Genre and form matter**: for plays, stage directions, staging, light and sound, interplay between characters. For poetry, form, lineation, sound.
- Precise textual knowledge: factual errors or vague references limit depth.
- Spelling, grammar and punctuation errors that detract from clarity cost marks. Plan briefly before writing.
