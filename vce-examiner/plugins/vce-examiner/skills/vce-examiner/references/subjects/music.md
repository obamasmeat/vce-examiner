# VCE Music (Inquiry, Composition, Contemporary Performance, Repertoire Performance)

Sources: VCAA exam specifications (written and performance), 2025 and 2024 external assessment reports. My own summary.
**This skill marks written aural/analysis answers and Statements of Expressive Intention. It can't hear a performance**, but it can critique a program plan or a written description against the criteria.

## Written exams
- **Inquiry / Composition:** 15 min reading + 1 hour writing. 75 marks. Aural excerpts and score-based analysis.
- **Contemporary / Repertoire Performance:** 15 min reading + 1 hour writing. Two sections, 60 marks.
- Terminology comes from the study design's **cross-study specifications** (compositional devices, music elements, concepts and language, around pp. 15–16). Use it precisely.

### Marking habits to replicate
- Answer with **all the required components** (e.g. tone colour *and* texture *and* a third element). Missing one caps the mark.
- Use the **named elements** in the question (e.g. contrast through duration + one other). Don't confuse **texture** with **tone colour**, and use vocabulary that belongs to the element you've chosen.
- **Specific, located evidence**: refer to the section or bar, the layer, the instrument and the actual rhythm or melody (notating rhythms like dotted quaver–semiquaver helps).
- **Repetition** means repeated musical material (a melodic motif, rhythm, riff). Continuing the same instrumentation or dynamic is *not* repetition.
- **Explain how** an element creates variation, transition or contrast. A general description of the excerpt scores low. Comments on affective outcomes can add cohesion.
- Only the first N responses are marked when N items are asked for (e.g. the first six instruments).
- Instrument identification: in order of entry when asked, with no repeats where excluded.
- Build element-specific vocabulary (mnemonics for each element's components help).

## Performance exams
- **Contemporary:** nine criteria, each out of 10, by two assessors. The program must meet compliance requirements (including a student **reimagining**, which can't be an existing cover or arrangement, or penalties apply), flagged on the Statement of Expressive Intention.
  - The Statement must describe the **character or mood** intended, not just stylistic changes.
  - High scorers: control and accuracy, authentic realisation of each style, varied dynamics, articulation and phrasing for expressive outcomes. Ensemble members need enough works to show a range of techniques.
- **Repertoire:** at least one Prescribed List work, and **more than 50% of the program on the nominated principal instrument**.
- Time limits: 20 minutes for one assessed performer, 25 minutes for two or three.
