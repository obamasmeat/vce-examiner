# VCE Art Creative Practice and Art Making and Exhibiting (Study Designs from 2023)

Sources: VCAA exam specifications, 2025 and 2024 external assessment reports. My own summary.

## Exam formats
Both: 15 min reading + 1 h 30 min writing. Section A (60): short and extended answer, often on colour visual stimulus in an insert. Section B (20): two extended answers or one essay.

## Art Creative Practice – marking habits
- **Interpretive Lenses** (Structural, Personal, Cultural, Contemporary) must be named and applied **consistently** throughout. When two lenses are asked for, give them equal attention.
- **Use the artwork as evidence**: refer to specific visual features (art elements and principles) rather than restating the written source information. Integrate sources with quotation marks or brackets and make your own inferences beyond them.
- Comparisons across artists (e.g. one contemporary and one historical) need **specific examples from every artwork discussed**. Prepared comparisons that ignore the question are penalised.
- Address *every* term in the question (e.g. "success" and "impermanence"). Multiple, developed comparisons.
- Art principles (e.g. movement) shown through specific locations in the work.

## Art Making and Exhibiting – marking habits
- **Discuss** materials, techniques and processes by linking each to **how meaning or visual language is constructed** (layering, texture, line). Lists of materials or processes stay in the low range.
- **Colour, mood, space**: explain purpose and impact, not just "gold, pink and purple". Use descriptive adjectives.
- **Exhibitions**: conservation, care and presentation methods must be *applied to the specific artwork or exhibition* (e.g. an outdoor organic sculpture). Rote lists of conservation practices score low. *Evaluate* purpose and effectiveness with examples.
- Curatorial tasks: justify the placement and presentation of chosen works (lighting, sightlines, themes, conservation needs).
- Ask yourself "What might this element suggest? How does it contribute to the overall message?"
- Legible handwriting. Structure answers to match the command term.
