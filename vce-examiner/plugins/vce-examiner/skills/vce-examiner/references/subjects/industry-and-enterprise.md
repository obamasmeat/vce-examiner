# VCE Industry and Enterprise

Sources: VCAA exam specifications, 2025 and 2024 external assessment reports. My own summary.

## Exam format
- 15 min reading + 2 hours writing. 70 marks, all compulsory. Short and extended answer with stimulus.
- The study design's "Characteristics of the study" (pp. 9–12) is examinable. Questions draw on the student's **structured workplace learning (SWL)**.

## Marking habits to replicate
- **Command terms** are a common failure point. *Compare* = similarities **and** differences (plural), explicitly described. Two descriptions joined by "whereas" is not a comparison. *Analyse* = a detailed, multi-element analysis. *To what extent* = address the degree.
- **Link explicitly**: enterprising behaviours → enterprise culture → workplace performance. Technology → the *development* of named work-related skills (and how much).
- Use **your SWL experience** with specific detail when asked. Describe behaviours *you* developed, not general workplace features.
- Use the stimulus as evidence when asked how or why something is enterprising.
- Answer on *enterprising approaches* to safety, not a general outline of safety.
- If continuing in the extra space, say so on the answer page.

## Content precision expected
- Enterprise culture and enterprising behaviours (adaptability, teamwork, problem solving, initiative), with examples.
- **Numerical flexibility** (varying the number of workers or hours) vs **functional flexibility** (multi-skilled workers in different roles). Correct misconceptions about each.
- A definition of technology as it applies to work tasks and processes.
- A **recent people-based innovation**: specific and detailed (more than half of students scored zero here).
- The role of government (local, state, Commonwealth) in industry change, with named policies or actions.
- Pressures and opportunities that create a need for change, linked to the stimulus.
