# Subject index

Each file in `subjects/` was built from VCAA's own exam specifications, expected-qualities/criteria documents and the two most recent external assessment (examiners') reports (mostly 2025 and 2024, downloaded October 2026). They're summaries in my own words, not official VCAA text.

**Before marking, read the file for the student's subject.** Treat it as what assessors actually reward and penalise. If the student supplies an official marking guide or report, that takes priority.

## Patterns that came up in nearly every report
Assessors in almost every subject flagged these. Use them as default checks:
1. **Not answering the exact question**: wrong focus, ignored qualifiers ("one", "other than", "both", plurals, timeframes), pre-prepared answers bent to fit.
2. **Restating the stem or stimulus** instead of adding knowledge or analysis.
3. **No link to the scenario/case study/stimulus**, or only name-dropping it.
4. **Command term ignored**: describing when asked to explain, no judgement when asked to evaluate, no similarities when asked to compare.
5. **Imprecise or non-study-design terminology**, and confusing paired concepts (equity/equality, efficiency/effectiveness, mitigation/adaptation).
6. **Vague effects without direction or mechanism** ("impacts life expectancy" instead of "decreases life expectancy because…").
7. **Units, working, exact values and rounding** in any quantitative subject.
8. **Legibility and answer placement**: scanned scripts, faint pencil, answers outside the boxes.
